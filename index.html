About

Quality engineering intern and manufacturing professional with hands-on experience in quality assurance, quality control and production operations.

At Miba Sinter USA, a high-volume sintered components plant serving automotive and industrial customers, I work within ISO 9001 quality systems using Statistical Process Control, GD&T drawing interpretation, ZEISS CMM dimensional inspection, metallography and microstructure analysis, hardness testing, product audits and PPAP to reduce variability and support continuous improvement.

Before Miba, I worked as a Chemical Blendman and Forklift Operator at Celanese (a contract role through DSV Solutions), producing polymer formulations to strict batch specifications, keeping accurate batch records and following SOPs. Safety training includes Hazmat Operations, Firefighter I and BLS.
